{% if step == 'welcome' %}

Welcome to Job Radar

Every night Job Radar searches job boards, company career pages and your job-alert e-mails across Europe, keeps the jobs that fit what you're looking for, and has an AI read each one: how well it fits you, a short summary and what to watch out for. In the morning the reviewed jobs are waiting in Notion, best first. It runs on GitHub's computers, free, so your computer doesn't need to stay on.

Setup takes about 10 minutes. Choose how much help you want; you can add the rest later.

{% elif step == 'notion' %}

Notion: where your jobs land

Every morning the reviewed jobs are waiting in a Notion database: fit, a short summary, what to watch out for and the full posting. Set a job's Status (Apply, Skip, Not relevant, Applied…) there, on your laptop or phone; the next night's run reads it. A free Notion account is enough.

{% if notion_connected %}

Connected{% if notion_url %}: open your Jobs database{% endif %}. Save to keep it, or paste another page to start a new database.

{% endif %}
  1. Open notion.so → Integrations, click New integration, name it “Job Radar”, pick your workspace, type Internal. Copy the Internal Integration Secret.
  2. In Notion, create an empty page (for example “Job search”). On that page: ••• → Connections → add Job Radar.
  3. Copy the page's link (Share → Copy link) and paste both below.

Job Radar creates a “Jobs” database on that page. Then add a view sorted by Fit (Notion can't create views for it): filter Status = New, sort Fit descending.

{% elif step == 'llm' %}

AI review

Each night an AI reads every new job and tells you how well it fits you (1-5), what the job is about, which languages and how much experience it asks for, and anything to watch out for. It runs on GitHub, so it needs an online AI service. Google's free plan is enough and never charges: it just stops at its daily limit, and the rest is reviewed the next night.

{% set cur = (llm.default or {}) %}

Easiest: Google Gemini (free)

  1. Open Google AI Studio ↗ and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and choose Create API key in new project, then copy the key. Don't click “Set up billing”: without it the key can't cost anything.
  3. Paste it below and click Test the key.

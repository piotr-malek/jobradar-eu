Job Radar

Every night GitHub fetches jobs from the boards you choose and matches them to your roles, in a private repo of your own. In the morning you open this app, pick the jobs worth applying to and apply. Nothing runs on your computer overnight.

You can switch from Lite to Full later (jr setup --upgrade); jobs and decisions are kept.
